The incumbent world remains authoritative for brand, tokens, navigation and protected motion. The requested three reading surfaces receive new compositions, rather than recoloring the old cards.

- About: Experience / Read. An author's letter, with a publication byline, large upright literary thesis, personal reading column, marginal AG mark, ordered working method and ethics colophon.

## System

Use existing --bg, --bg-raised, --text, --muted(-strong), --orange, --line(-strong), --orange-line, --literary and --shell. No new palette, icon library, font service or effects package. Serif at editorial display/lead; existing sans at metadata/body/actions. Rules mark publication/record boundaries, not decoration around every paragraph. One clear reading edge; controlled asymmetry at desktop, source-order stack on mobile. Native links and details. Text remains present without JavaScript; no entry animation is needed for reading content.

## Alternative decision

Two runnable, QA-only composition families were produced. A: author's letter + dominant cover publication spread (chosen). B: working notebook + compact ledger (scripts/v6-qa/variants). Both use real copy and the same assets; neither is just a palette variation. B is denser and less personal, so A better expresses the requested personal documentary publication. No visitor-facing variation switch or new feature was added.

Taste's actual Node layout dealer returned word and statement for a photo-free brief. Its business-only categories, fixed screen quotas, forced photo quantities, rotating words and ban on image captions conflict with this documentary/reading brief and were not copied. The retired editorial recipe was identified and rejected. Impeccable source findings are reviewed against actual cascade and protected brand choices, never treated as automatic truth.

## Media state

A real future portrait can replace the marginal mark using .author-portrait without an empty slot today. A figure uses a real 4:5 picture, dimensions/srcset/alt and reviewed EXIF. Actual research stills belong beside the corresponding source or record, and are never independent proof of a self-report. No photo-ready class causes a broken image or blank placeholder in the product.

## Release boundary
