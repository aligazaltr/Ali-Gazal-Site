## Specificity verdict

The product premise is specific; the current composition only partly expresses it. The honest distinction between idea, personal test, self-report, and independent evidence is unusually strong. Orange/graphite, warm type, Machiavelli opening, and AG rings supply a coherent identity. But About is still a familiar creator manifesto (giant statement, three method columns, principles, CTA), Projects is a feature card plus a teaser, and the dossier becomes a stack of administrative panels. These could house another thoughtful creator after changing copy. V6 should make the publication and investigation themselves determine the layouts.

## Cognitive load and emotional journey

About: **1/8 checklist failures**, progressive disclosure/rhythm. Its content is digestible, but every section announces rather than develops the story. Projects: **1/8**, hierarchy/one clear selection suffers because the introduction is a large first act before the usable index. Dossier: **4/8**, high load: single focus, chunking, one thing at a time, minimal choices. Grouping, local legibility, recognition, and access to deeper detail largely work. The TOC offers **9 simultaneous choices**; protocol has 5 equal rules; the end bridge lists 5 related thoughts plus the archive CTA. Header navigation itself has only four items and is manageable.

Emotional peak: the portrait/title/cover opening earns cinematic anticipation. Valley: the visitor then receives facts → phase dashboard → status notice → 9-link TOC → share action before the actual investigation. The next three sections ask closely related questions. Later, dense evidence cards demand stamina while the sticky side card repeatedly announces an unavailable video. The end underplays the meaningful finding: “DENEYİN KISA SONUCU” is visually small while earlier questions are huge, and five boxed limitations dominate it. Honesty reassures, but repetition makes the work feel defensive. Preserve every limitation while organizing them once, in context.

## Strengths to preserve

1. Clear and unusually credible separation of experiment completion, video publication, self-report, and independent proof. Do not exchange this for impressive statistics or invented documentation.
2. AG rings, warm paper/graphite/orange, serif emphasis, and Machiavelli portrait-to-cover opening create an identifiable editorial vocabulary.
3. Three behavioral rules, chronological evidence dates, and “Desteklediği / Kanıtlamadığı” fields give a real documentary backbone. They should drive the V6 composition.

## Prioritized concrete issues

1. **P1 — Dossier narrative is hidden behind duplicated status infrastructure.** Immediately after the protected opening, use a publication masthead with source/date/status, then a prominent two-sentence honest finding (“olumlu fakat kısmi”), a single caveat, and four chapter links: Soru / Üç ilke / Kayıtlar / Sonuç ve sınırlar. Consolidate phase + truth + video notices into one factual rail. Preserve all existing prose/evidence below; no fake video CTA.
2. **P1 — Projects lacks a publication index and truth-aligned taxonomy.** Replace “Aktif proje / 1 dosya” + huge card + separate teaser with numbered editorial entries. Machiavelli = completed experiment, video pending; Payot = reading/research, test not begun. Let status and source be scannable beside each title. Include Marcus only as an explicitly labeled thought-archive path, never as a third conducted project. Remove the unsupported “rehberler” promise from visible title unless actual guides exist.
3. **P2 — About tells the same manifesto repeatedly without visual development.** The opening is effective, but “Sadece anlatmak değil…”, “Bilmekle uygulamak…”, and “Sonuç neyse…” repeat the same sans/orange-italic sentence composition. Recompose as an author’s editorial letter with a personal byline, a visible central question, a continuous method sequence, and a compact ethics colophon. Photo-free should look intentionally complete; no empty portrait reservation.
4. **P2 — Dossier gives questions more authority than findings.** Merge or tighten the visual treatment of Başlangıç sorunu / Deney sorusu / Neden; maintain the factual copy but use one clear question headline and smaller supporting sections. Promote actual finding to a strong editorial pull statement. Present 4 records as dated ledger rows/chapters with compact metadata and legible detail rather than another set of generic dark cards; keep B/C explanations nearby.
5. **P2 — Craft details interrupt documentary credibility.** About method ↗/↘ and external-arrow positions visibly render as missing-glyph rectangles in this browser. Replace with reliable inline SVG or CSS shapes. Remove question-mark decoration from factual limitation bullets; it reads as uncertainty about the caveat itself. Check motion reveal: immediately after scroll the About section was briefly entirely empty before content appeared, so reading content should remain promptly visible and reduced-motion safe.

## Two meaningfully different composition families

**A — Author’s letter + numbered publication shelf (recommended).** About opens with a small “Ali Gazal / kişisel çalışma notu” byline and one large serif thesis, then an asymmetric two-column editorial letter: wide narrative on the left, narrow numbered margin notes on the right. A full-width horizontal Fikir → Deney → Sonuç line is the structural hinge; the closing is a compact ethics colophon plus one current-work link. Projects becomes a crisp issue index: narrow serial/date/source/status columns, large title/question, one Mach cover alongside the completed issue, Payot as a text-led research entry, and Marcus as a separate archive footnote. This creates a human publication, not a portfolio template.

**B — Working notebook + production table.** About uses a split spread: one fixed-size AG diagram/large initial and a short “Neyi araştırıyorum?” statement, followed by a ruled working notebook of question → action → evaluation. Ethics appear as a margin strip rather than another slogan section. Projects becomes a documentary production ledger, one substantial row per work, with four explicit state columns (Kaynak / Çalışma / Kayıt / Yayın). Machiavelli has the existing cover and precise completion/video labels; Payot has a typographic source treatment and research status; Marcus sits below the project ledger under “Düşünce Arşivi.” No progress percentages, fake dates, or fabricated media. More analytical and less intimate than A.

## Persona red flags

- **Jordan, first-time viewer:** “Aktif proje” + completed test creates avoidable uncertainty about what can be read or watched. About answers the premise quickly but sends the first concrete work near its end.
- **Casey, distracted mobile reader:** the long preamble and nine-link TOC are likely to delay the useful finding. Mobile layout itself was not inspected, so this is a task-flow concern, not a claim of mobile breakage.
- **Riley, skeptical reader:** publishing an experiment invites a distinction between observation and proof. Existing boundaries are excellent; maintain them. Pending source references should remain explicit, and stages should not imply a published video. Repetition of caveats should be consolidated rather than removed.

Minor observations: preview environment bar is operational chrome, not a permanent editorial design element. Preserve its truth while keeping it visually subordinate. The lower archive bridge supplies worthwhile continuation but should have one dominant archive action rather than six equal exits.
