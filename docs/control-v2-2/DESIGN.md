# V2.2 visual prototype

V2's nine depth frames created spatial travel; V2.1 removed that geometry and left only a subtle radial field. V2.1 also hid the original body light/texture beneath a project-wide flat fill. Those are rejected choices.

Restore the original body background without editing styles.css. Make the sticky stage transparent so its last frame and the dossier share the actual page background. No duplicated background tiles or flat page override.

Prototype: three open material planes (left, right, floor) at distinct depths. Their near/far corners project from the same moving zero centre. A single camera moves through them; they are filled surfaces with partial warm inner edges, not closed frame rings. Warm reflected light accompanies geometry; it is not the only moving feature. The world fades as the approved question opens. Keep V2.1 native clipping, baked numeral contours and transparent metadata labels.

This is not declared visually accepted or technically complete until the browser comparison covers 20/30/40/50/60/70 percent on phone and desktop in both themes. Unit-test/quality pass comes afterwards. V2, V2.1 and V2.2 are available in the same isolated review app.
